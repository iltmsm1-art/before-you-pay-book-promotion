Before You Pay for Book Promotion

A practical decision guide for independent authors.

Independent authors receive a steady stream of offers promising exposure, readers, visibility, reviews, publicity, and promotion.

The difficult question isn't whether the offer sounds good.

Is there enough behind the promise to justify spending your money?

Before You Pay for Book Promotion is a short decision memo designed to help you slow down the sales pitch, examine what's actually being offered, and decide what deserves further investigation.

Use it before you buy

The guide helps you examine promotional claims, identify information that's missing, recognize questions worth asking, and reach one of three simple conclusions:

PROCEED   |   INVESTIGATE   |   PASS

No promises about promises

This isn't another book-promotion service.

It doesn't promise sales, rankings, reviews, bestseller status, or thousands of new readers.

It's simply a practical tool for independent authors who want to make a more informed decision before paying for promotion.
